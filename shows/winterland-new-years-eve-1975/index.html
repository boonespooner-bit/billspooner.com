Winterland Ballroom

San Francisco, California · December 31, 1975
  • The TubesAct 1
  • Flo & EddieAct 2

Six months after opening for Kiss in the same room, The Tubes closed out 1975 headlining it — their debut album out, “White Punks on Dope” already a local anthem. KSAN broadcast the whole thing live across the Bay Area, and the tape survives.
