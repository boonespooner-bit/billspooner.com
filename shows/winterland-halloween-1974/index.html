Winterland Ballroom

San Francisco, California · October 31, 1974

Billed as a Halloween Masquerade Ball. Bill Graham had been wary of the band after Kezar; he got over it, and Halloween at Winterland became a Tubes institution — the one night of the year when a stage show built on costume, spectacle and bad taste needed no explaining to anybody.
