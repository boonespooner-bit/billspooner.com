Knebworth Park

Billed, gloriously, as “Oh God, Not Another Boring Old Knebworth.” Zappa and The Tubes were announced as joint headliners — the official programme puts both names on one line, at the same size. The running order printed inside settles who finished the night: Wilko Johnson, Rockpile, the Boomtown Rats, Peter Gabriel, Frank Zappa, and then The Tubes, closing the festival in front of roughly 45,000 people. Peter Gabriel spent his set climbing the stage scaffolding with a large stuffed panda. One of the great strange bills of the decade, and the high-water mark of the band’s standing in Britain.
